Sultanpur Cricket Club was born in 2026 with a simple belief — cricket is more than just a game. It is about teamwork, discipline, respect and the passion that brings people together.

From our first steps, our goal has been to create a club where players can represent their team with pride, develop their abilities and build something meaningful together.
